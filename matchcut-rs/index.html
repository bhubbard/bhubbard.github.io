matchcut-rs — Automated Visual, Motion & Acoustic Match-Cut Discovery in Rust

matchcut-rs is a high-speed Rust port of Netflix's matchcut engine that scans video libraries to find seamless graphic, motion, and audio match cuts between scenes. It computes perceptual frame embeddings, optical-flow velocity fields, and onset envelopes to rank smooth transition pairs in milliseconds.
