adforge-rs — Automated 6-Track Commercial Assembly & Legal Compliance Engine

adforge-rs is a pure Rust broadcast assembly engine for generating compliant television, social, and streaming video commercials from script to master render. It combines 6-track non-linear timeline mixing, EBU R128 loudness normalization, and automated California SB 37 legal disclaimer verification.
